# Legacy-Voice
### Preserve your words forever

##What it does
LegacyVoice transforms personal memories and jot notes into beautifully written heartfelt letters; elegantly formatted and read aloud sing ElevenLabs AI voice technology.

## The problem it solves
Words fade. Voices are forgotten. LegacyVoice preserves both, turning simple memories into lasting keepsakes you can hear, download as a PDF, and share forever. It also helps make heartfelt letters to anyone, even when you do not know the right words to add.

## How it Works
1. Enter who your letter is addressed to and who is writing said letter
2. Write your memory or even just jot notes
3. Click "Create your letter"
4. Read the beautifully AI-written letter
5. Hear it aloud
6. Download it as a PDF to keep forever.


## Technologies Used
- Base44 - app building and hosting
- ElevenLabs - AI voice generation
- AI letter writing - transforms raw memories into elegant heartfelt letters
